Clover — the monoline mark

Four open petals drawn as a single stroke weight. It reads as a four-leaf clover and sits naturally beside the Lucide line-icons in the product. The stroke thickens slightly as the mark shrinks so it stays legible down to the favicon.

construction C1 · monoline · accent #4ADE80

The mark

96px · on dark / on light

Size ladder

16 → 160px · adaptive stroke

Stroke weight is specified in the mark’s own 48-unit space and scales up at small sizes: 4u at 16px down to 2.1u at 160px. This keeps the line from disappearing as a favicon while staying elegant in the marketing hero.

Construction notes

spec
leaves  4 open petals, 12·3·6·9
fill    none — stroke only
stroke  #4ADE80 on dark · #08080A on light
weight  2.1–4u (size-adaptive)
joins   round
center  open cross of negative space
stem   omitted
Do
— keep a single, even stroke weight
— let it share weight with Lucide icons
— pair with Inter Medium wordmark

Don’t
— fill the petals or add a second color
— thin the stroke below 16px legibility
— rotate or skew the mark

Named for Dr. Clover, head archaeologist of Ohara — who died trying to share suppressed knowledge with the world.
