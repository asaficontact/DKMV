Clover mark — round two

The first geometric petal read as crowded — four fat leaves with almost no breathing room. These nine keep the confident Clover green but open up the negative space. Each is shown large, then at 24px and 16px so you can judge how it holds when it gets small (where crowding bites hardest).

A · Quatrefoil family

circle-union · architectural

Four overlapping circles. The cusps between lobes form naturally — this is the most "clover silhouette" reading, and it nods to the gothic quatrefoil (scholarly, archival — fitting for Dr. Clover). Generous and calm, never crowded.

B · Open petals

slimmer leaves · real gaps

The original direction, but the leaves are narrowed and pulled apart so the center cross of negative space actually reads. Lighter on the page, especially small.

C · Line & aperture

monoline · pinwheel

Stroke-based and rotational takes. The monoline reads as a line-icon (Lucide-adjacent, sits naturally beside the UI icons); the aperture spins the leaves into a shutter — most abstract, most "tool".
